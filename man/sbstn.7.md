<h2>NAME</h2>
<p>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;sbstn - developer philosophy, mindset and principles
</p>

<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;How I think about building software, and about taking it apart.
</p>

<h2>CORE PRINCIPLES</h2>
<p>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Clarity over cleverness - future me has to read this.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Simplicity as a strategy - complexity grows by itself.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Trust nothing - validate input, verify assumptions, check the logs.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Attack your own work - if I can break it, so can someone else.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Curiosity as fuel - learning never stops.
</p>

<h2>WORKFLOW</h2>
<p>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Start small. Move, measure, adjust.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Automate the second time, not the fifth.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Fail early - errors are information.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Ship often - small releases hurt less.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Patch first, blog later.
</p>

<h2>MINDSET</h2>
<p>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Calm over chaos.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Defaults are a suggestion.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Ownership of everything I touch.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Security is a habit, not a feature.
</p>
