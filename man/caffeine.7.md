<h2>NAME</h2>
<p>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;caffeine - unofficial dependency
</p>

<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Hard runtime dependency of sbstn(1). Not vendored, pulled fresh<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;several times a day. No lockfile.
</p>

<h2>VERSIONS</h2>
<p>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;black - stable<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;espresso - hotfix<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;cold - legacy, found on the desk next morning
</p>

<h2>KNOWN ISSUES</h2>
<p>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Without it: reduced throughput, shorter commit messages.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;With too much of it: refactoring at 2 am. Results vary.
</p>

<h2>SECURITY CONSIDERATIONS</h2>
<p>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Single point of failure. No mitigation planned.
</p>
